# PushPal — Vision

## What Is PushPal?

PushPal is a cross-platform scheduled push notification service between trusted people. It is the missing layer between messaging and reminders.

## Core Problem

No tool lets you schedule a push notification to someone else's phone and know they saw it. Messaging apps are real-time. Reminder apps are for yourself. PushPal bridges that gap.

## Primary Users (MVP)

- **Couples** — "Remind me to take out the trash at 7pm" / "Remind my partner to call the dentist"
- **Families** — "Remind my kid to do homework at 4pm"

## Core Beliefs

1. **Speed over features** — Schedule a reminder in under 5 seconds
2. **Trust through visibility** — Know when a notification was sent, delivered, viewed, and completed
3. **Earn the native app** — Prove value with a PWA first, then invest in native

## Success Criteria

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Time to schedule a self-reminder | < 5 seconds |
| Time to send to someone else | < 10 seconds |
| Push notification delivery rate | > 99% |

## 12-Month Vision

| Phase | Months | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| MVP | 1–3 | Core functionality: auth, self-reminders, couple linking, send to partner |
| Public Launch | 4–6 | Polish, reliability, onboarding, early users |
| Native Apps | 7–9 | iOS and Android native experiences |
| Team Features | 10–12 | Groups, recurring, advanced scheduling |
